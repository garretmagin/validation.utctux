First Discovery: A 24-Hour Testpass

Before

Device Pool Bottleneck

One testpass was running up to 24 hours — not because of test complexity, but because a dedicated device pool lacked capacity, causing massive queuing delays.

→
After

General Automation Pool

Moved the AutoPlus testpasses to a standard shared automation pool with available capacity. The bottleneck disappeared.

The visualization made it obvious — one bar stretched across the entire chart. Without it, this would have stayed hidden in aggregate metrics.
